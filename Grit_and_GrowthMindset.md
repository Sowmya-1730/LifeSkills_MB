## Introduction to Growth Mindset
### Paraphrase the video in 1 to 2 lines in your own words
There will be two types of mindsets. One is fixed mindset and another is Growth mindset.
- Fixed Mindset: People who have this kind of mindset will always tend to think that skills are born, we cannot improve, grow and always ready to back away from challenges.
- Growth Mindset: People who have this kind of mindset will always tend to think that skills are to built, we can grow by learning, by taking feedbacks, creating challenges to improve further.
There are two main things that differentiate between Fixed mindset people and Growth mindset people are BELIEF and FOCUS
