## Tiny Habits by Bj Fogg - Core Message
#### How can you use B = MAP to make new habits easier? What are M, A, and P?
In B = MAP, 
- B stands for Behaviour
- M stands for Motivation
- A stands for ability
- P stands for Prompt
We use this formula, on to make new habits easier means by to achieve a behaviour we will take the tasks in a very simple way to stay motivated. How simple it was we can stay less focused by that. And you need to make sure that the task to do is in your ability. Make the prompt very easy so you will always tend to do that task to achieve your behaviour.


#### Why it is important to "Shine" or Celebrate after each successful completion of habit? (This is the most important concept in today's topic. Whatever you celebrate becomes a habit)
After every successful completion of habit, celebrating will always help us to stay motivated and lead us to achieve our required things.

## Book Summary of Atomic Habits
#### What is the book's perspective about Identity?
This book's perspective about identity-based approach to habit change, arguing that altering how we see ourselves is crucial for achieving lasting behaviour changes and meaningful growth.


#### Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?
In this book, there are multiple strategies on how to make a habit easier to do. In that, the strategy that I liked is Two-minute rule. To achieve that habit, try to do it in 2 minutes for every day.


#### Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?
To achieve some things in your life, you need to put effort on it. For suppose, you want to reduce your screen time. So, you need to keep the phone in separate room and do your things. It need effort to do, which makes it harder to achieve.
